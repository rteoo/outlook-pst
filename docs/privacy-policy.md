# Outlook PST/OST privacy policy

Effective date: 2026-10-29.

Publisher: RODRIGO TEODORO ALVES (GitHub: rteoo).
This policy covers the Outlook PST/OST plugin and its bundled local Python CLI.

## Information processed

At your direction, the CLI reads local PST/OST archives or items available in
Classic Outlook. These can include message bodies, headers, sender and recipient
details, attachments, dates, folder names, and message identifiers. The CLI uses
this information to search, display, export, or perform the changes you request.

## Local execution and other services

The bundled CLI has no publisher-operated mail-upload endpoint, telemetry, or
analytics. It writes requested exports to your chosen local destination and does
not send email. The publisher does not receive your mail through the CLI.

When you use the plugin through an AI assistant, prompts, command output, and mail
content can enter that assistant's context. The assistant platform's processing,
storage, and retention rules apply. Running the CLI locally does not mean all
information shown to an assistant stays on your computer.

Classic Outlook and your email provider can synchronize mailbox changes with
servers and other devices. Dependency downloads and GitHub visits also contact
third parties. Their respective terms and privacy policies apply to those services.

## Storage and deletion

You control the locations of your archives and exports. Exports persist until you
remove them; the plugin does not automatically expire them. Removing the plugin
does not remove archives or exports. Manage assistant conversations and provider-side
mailbox copies through the relevant provider's controls.

## Support information and contact

For support or questions about this policy, use
<https://github.com/rteoo/outlook-pst/issues>. Issues are public and hosted by
GitHub. Information you submit there is available to the publisher and others;
GitHub's privacy and retention practices apply to its hosted records. The publisher
uses information submitted in issues to respond and investigate reported problems.

Do not include real mail, attachments, credentials, personal addresses, or account
names in public issues. Use fictional or redacted examples. To request a private
channel, post only a minimal request without confidential information. For security
reports, follow [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md).

You can use the same minimal-contact route to ask about support information you
have submitted or request its removal. GitHub controls its platform records and
available deletion mechanisms; no particular deletion deadline is promised here.

## Updates

Changes to this policy will be published in this repository with a revised
effective date. Git history records previous versions.
